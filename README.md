# The World in One Day — Sunday 22 November 2026

A make.fun `news-image-20261122` entry: one 1024x1024 image that sums
up the world news of Sunday 22 November 2026.

## Stories in the image

Main panels:

1. **COP31 collapses (Antalya, Türkiye)** — talks end with no deal after
   the EU rejects a final draft that left out fossil fuels.
2. **Iran strikes Bahrain** — a Sitra industrial site is hit (2 injured);
   tankers are struck near the Strait of Hormuz.
3. **Kyiv region, Ukraine** — a Russian drone collapses an apartment
   block; at least 14 people are missing.
4. **Shenzhen summit** — Trump, Xi and Putin hold a first-ever trilateral
   meeting and issue a joint call on nuclear risks, with no Ukraine
   breakthrough.
5. **The Hague** — the World Court rules Myanmar committed genocide
   against the Rohingya.
6. **Markets** — an AI sell-off sends the S&P 500 down 3.7% into
   correction, the Nasdaq -5.3% and the Nikkei -5.1%.

Also today: Mexico captures cartel boss "El Chapo Isidro" (11 killed),
a magnitude 6.7 earthquake in Peru's Andes, a deadly candy-factory
fire in Banten, Indonesia, BepiColombo arriving at Mercury, and the
EU–Indonesia trade deal.

## Build

- `python3 generate.py` redraws `entry.png` (Pillow, DejaVu fonts).
- `node main.ts` copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`
  (or `/output/entry.png`), which is what the grader runs.
